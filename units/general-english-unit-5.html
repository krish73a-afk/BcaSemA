🗣️ GENERAL ENGLISH - I · UNIT V

Writing practice

This unit combines reading comprehension, precis writing and essays. The common skill is keeping the central meaning: discover what matters, then express it clearly in fewer or better-organized words.

PICTURE IT

A precis is a tiny suitcase: it carries only the most important ideas from a big passage.

Imagine packing a small travel bag. Keep what the trip needs, leave out what is extra, and make important things easy to find.

BY THE END, YOU CAN

  • Identify a passage's main idea, details, tone and meaning from context.
  • Write a precis in your own words without changing the source's point.
  • Plan and draft a focused essay under a time limit.
✧

LESSON PATH

Build the idea step by step

Read one card, say it back in your own words, then try the example.

01

Read for the whole message before details

For comprehension, read once to understand the message and again to mark its main idea and support. Notice the author's tone and use nearby words to infer unfamiliar vocabulary. Answer what the passage says, not a related idea you already know.

02

A precis keeps the argument and removes extras

A precis is a shorter version that preserves a passage's central idea, logic and important points. Remove repetition, illustrations and minor details, but do not change the author's claim. Use your own words, keep the order logical, remain objective, and follow the given word limit.

03

An essay needs a focused path

Unpack the prompt and choose one clear position or main claim. Sketch an introduction with a thesis, body paragraphs with one point and an example each, and a conclusion that follows from the argument. Save time to check sentence boundaries and whether every paragraph answers the prompt.

✦

WORK IT THROUGH

Plan a short essay on responsible technology use.

  1. Thesis: technology helps learning when used thoughtfully.
  2. Point 1: it gives access to explanations; example: replay a lesson. Point 2: distractions and misinformation need boundaries; example: verify sources and silence alerts.
  3. Conclusion: use technology with a purpose and check its information.

WATCH OUT FOR

Easy mix-ups

  • A precis does not add your opinion.
  • A precis is not just the first sentence copied from every paragraph.
  • An essay plan should answer the exact prompt, not the whole topic.

EXAM ANSWER BUILDER

Show your thinking

  1. Start with the key definition or rule.
  2. Show each step, label and example clearly.
  3. Finish with the result and a one-line reason.

If the question says “compare,” write both sides using the same points.

CORE IDEAS TO REMEMBER
01

For comprehension, identify the main idea, supporting details, tone and the meaning of words from context.

02

For a precis, read twice, identify the thesis, remove examples and repetition, use your own words and retain the logical order.

03

A precis is substantially shorter than the source; follow any word limit or ratio stated in the exam.

04

An essay needs a focused thesis, connected paragraphs, examples and a conclusion that follows from the argument.

IMPORTANT QUESTIONS · TAP TO REVEAL MODEL ANSWERS
QUESTION 1What are the steps for writing a precis?+

Read the passage once for its overall message and again to mark the main claim and supporting points. Remove illustrations, repeated ideas and minor details. Draft a shorter version in your own words while preserving the original meaning and order. Check that it is objective, coherent and within the required length. Add a suitable title if asked. Do not add an opinion absent from the passage.

QUESTION 2How should a short essay be planned under time pressure?+

Spend a few minutes identifying the prompt's key term and choosing one clear position. Make a three-part outline: introduction and thesis, two or three body points with examples, and conclusion. Write one main idea per paragraph. Leave time to check sentence boundaries, agreement, punctuation and whether each paragraph answers the prompt.
